The Face-Grain Board

Handcrafted from the finest materials, these boards offer a perfectly smooth surface that is gentle on your knives while proudly displaying the premium timber's natural grain. Built to last a lifetime, we strictly use dense, kiln-dried hardwoods that naturally resist warping, cracking, and staining. With proper care, your cutting board will retain its striking beauty and remain a treasured staple in your kitchen. We also craft custom boards in a variety of sizes and thicknesses tailored to your specific needs—whether for heavy-duty food preparation, serving as an elegant charcuterie tray, or simply acting as a beautiful piece of kitchen décor.

The Integrated Slicer

Our custom cheese slicers bridge the gap between convenience and elegance. Featuring a built-in, high-tension stainless steel wire, these boards allow you to achieve uniform, deli-style slices—from paper-thin ribbons of Gruyère to thick wedges of Sharp Cheddar—without the struggle of a traditional knife.

Common Inquiries

We specialize in premium, professional-grade custom cutting boards, cheese-slicers and charcuterie displays, and edge-grain butcher blocks.

Casa Designs uses food-safe, non-toxic oils to finish pieces that come into contact with food or skin. These finishes enhance the natural beauty of the wood while providing a protective barrier against moisture and wear, ensuring that your cutting boards and charcuterie pieces remain safe and stunning for years to come.

Hand wash with soapy warm water, Dry thoroughly. DO NOT soak in water, or put into dishwasher. Periodically rub with food safe mineral oil to keep the wood from drying out and allow to dry completely before storing.
